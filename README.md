# HTL Labs, Projects, and Reports

This repository is a digitized collection and archive of selected projects, laboratory reports, and technical coursework from my time at technical high school (German: *HTL*).

Although only part of the original material could be recovered from older backup drives, the repository provides a representative view of the foundational hands-on experience gained during that period. The archived material spans topics such as PLC programming, industrial automation, electrical engineering lab work, and robotic simulation projects.

## Contents

- `Electrical_Lab/` – selected reports from electronics and electrical engineering laboratory work
- `IndustrialAutomationPLC_Lab/` – selected reports and project material from industrial automation and PLC coursework
- `Robotic_Lab/` – screen recordings of RobotStudio simulations for robot programming and motion-control exercises

## Note

This repository serves as an archive of early technical work and documentation from my HTL education and is not intended to be a complete reconstruction of all original projects and reports.
